Stratum Industries Tashkent Founded 2026

GovAI is built by Stratum Industries, a Y Combinator company based in Tashkent. We started with a simple observation: Uzbekistan's public servants do some of the most important work in the country, and they do it with software that was built before AI existed.

So we built GovAI — a secure AI workspace that drafts, researches, and translates the work, so staff only need to review and approve it. It runs entirely on local government infrastructure, because sovereignty here isn't a feature. It's the whole point.

The government can't run on paper speed.

We're not asking anyone to take that on faith. GovAI is already piloting with Quyi-Chirchiq District Hokimiyat, where 50 public servants have used it — and 90% of them said they want AI in their daily work.
